One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 — a drawn front page of the day's biggest stories: illustrated main stories with region and topic tags, a world map with a pin for every story, a numbered "also today" tier covering six continents, a "Day in Numbers" strip of the day's hardest figures, stories-by-region tallies, and a quote of the day. Every story was verified against wire and broadcaster reporting of 18 November 2026 (Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera and others); disputed claims are attributed in the image itself. The poster is drawn programmatically with Pillow (draw.py); the entry program main.py is standard-library only and copies the committed entry.png byte-for-byte to /output/entry.png, verifying its size and signature.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
